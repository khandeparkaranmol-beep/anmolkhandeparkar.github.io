The experience

01 · Learn

One fold at a time.

Watch the movement, listen to the instruction, and try it with real paper. Replay a tricky step as often as you need.

Optional tabletop AR places the demonstration beside your paper.

02 · Celebrate

You made that.

Finish the last fold and give your creation a little personality. Each digital model has its own scene, movement, and sound.

Save a keepsake of the digital creation.

03 · Keep going

A small beginning.
A whole collection.

Start with approachable creations, then work towards more involved models. Saved lesson progress and badges mark the journey.

A closer look

How I built it

Designed around
real paper.

The phone demonstrates. The learner makes. I built the experience around small, replayable steps, clear visual checkpoints, and a playful reward for finishing.

A native SwiftUI app with RealityKit folding scenes, ARKit table placement, and bundled narration. Lesson positions and achievements stay on the iPhone, so practice can continue without an account.

The camera places the demonstration; it does not grade physical folds. Real-paper testing is ongoing. Paper School is not yet on the App Store.
