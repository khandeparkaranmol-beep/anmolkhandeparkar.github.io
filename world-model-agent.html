Design Decisions

Why a world model?
The goal is an agent that learns action consequences from experience and uses imagined trajectories to improve its policy. This is a compact Dreamer-style implementation, not a claim of exact DreamerV3 parity.
What counts as progress?
Better prediction loss or training-action agreement is not enough. Real gameplay, held-out situations, retained learning, and limited-exploration adaptation need separate evidence.

Current Status

Active research. The learning system is integrated; reliable level completion and adaptation to unfamiliar games remain the next capability goals.
